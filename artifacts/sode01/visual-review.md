## Assessment

The revised pair is materially better than the initial result the user criticized. Its main plate field now carries the established Dō's woven lacing, recessed holes, small fittings and restrained lacquer wear. Short woven sections separated by dark lacquer have replaced both the empty metal-strip appearance and the stretched red slots of the intermediate texture crop. The current pair reads as part of the existing Kabuto/Dō set in actual Unreal images.

The shoulder outline remains recognizable: five curved courses, the asymmetric crowned header, a bounded lower edge and narrow brass details. The revision improves construction and finish without introducing a new armor design. This is a visual judgement about the inspected result; it does not establish that the user has accepted the revised quality.

## Residual limitations

1. Repeated atlas motifs and evenly spaced lamellae remain more regular than the reference sheets' dense, varied handmade construction. Close views still show a procedural rhythm. The improvement is substantial, but the result does not reproduce every reference detail or its photographic finish.
2. The suspension cords form conspicuous arcs above the shoulders. Their attachment relationship is less visually compact than in the reference mannequin panels, especially in close and oblique views. Thin cord endpoints are difficult to judge from a single shaded angle.
3. The raised-arm and bow-diagnostic panels take a visibly lifted, wing-like position. This preserves rigid clearance but is a simplified motion treatment; these stills do not demonstrate gravity-driven hanging or secondary motion.
4. The lining is deliberately regular, and the medallion/knots remain simplified relative to the ornate reference close-ups.

The paired renders support visual consistency; image appearance alone cannot prove semantic left/right naming. The manifest identifies `Sode_L_01` as the wearer's left, native Blender +X, and `Sode_R_01` as the wearer's right, native Blender -X. Import and handedness validation remain the responsibility of the pipeline checks.
