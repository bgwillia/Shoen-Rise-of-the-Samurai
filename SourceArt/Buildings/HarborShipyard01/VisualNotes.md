The revised exterior includes the taller open frame, projecting shingled gable, reused thatch roof, straight/end dock modules, slipway, hand winch, mooring posts, net rack, working ropes, existing lumber stacks and crates. `WorkBoat_01` is one reusable design, instanced under the shed and at the dock. Existing rural materials are retained; timber vertex color varies at the waterline. Static sea and sparse coastal dressing are review assets, with no maritime gameplay.

Unreal assets: `/Game/Art/Buildings/Maritime/HarborShipyard01/`. Saved map: `Review/HarborShipyard01_Settlement`. Harbor origin: (3200, 5600, 0) cm, on the clear southern settlement edge. The original settlement map is preserved.

Obvious unresolved visual issues: the shoreline is still too straight, the Unreal water remains visually flat, and the shared materials lack the reference's fine weathering and surface richness. The net also loses definition at distance. This is a revised art candidate, not a claim of reference-quality finish or user approval.
