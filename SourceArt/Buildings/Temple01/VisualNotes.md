# Temple_01

Editable source: `Temple01.blend`. Exterior modules include the hall, sweeping shingle roof, open veranda, railing, stone footings/steps, roofed gate, bell pavilion/bell, curtains and low fence. Lanterns reuse SmallShrine_01; planting reuses Manor_01. The refined cedar uses the existing timber shader arrangement with one new texture set; provenance is in `Textures/Generated/Provenance.txt`.

Unreal: `/Game/Art/Buildings/Religion/Temple01/Review/Temple01_Settlement`. The temple is north of the manor, facing the village. Existing settlement actors are preserved. The combined mesh is `SM_Temple_01`; the level uses the modular pieces with simple exterior collision.

Visual limits: fine carvings and natural wear remain simpler than the reference. The surrounding settlement terrain and lighting are still prototype presentation. No interiors or gameplay were added.
