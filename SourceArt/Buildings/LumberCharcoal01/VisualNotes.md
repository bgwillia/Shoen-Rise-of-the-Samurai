Authored modules include the open shed, lean-to, earthen kiln, lumber rack, three base logs, log pile, chopping block/axe, work trestle, charcoal storage and dirt apron. No interiors or production gameplay.

Unreal: `/Game/Art/Buildings/Production/LumberCharcoal01/`. The grouped yard is placed beside Smithy_01 at the settlement edge in `Review/LumberCharcoal01_Settlement`. Open `Launch_LumberCharcoal01.command` to inspect it. This is an art settlement map; the default gameplay map is unchanged.

FBX exports use linear vertex colors to preserve the authored finish in Unreal.
