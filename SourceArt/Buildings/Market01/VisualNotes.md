Market_01 is an exterior-only modular trading area, with a thatched pavilion, three reusable side stalls, tied canvas awnings, tables, eight goods modules, open fencing and a market banner. Timber, thatch, stone, rope, baskets and ground dressing reuse the SHŌEN rural kit.

Remaining visual limitations: the thatch courses and canvas still look more orderly than the reference's hand-worn surfaces; repeated goods are noticeable close up. The reference's full photoreal finish has not been reached.
