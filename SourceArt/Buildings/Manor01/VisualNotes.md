# Manor_01

Editable exterior source: `Manor01.blend`. Fifteen mesh assets are imported into `/Game/Art/Buildings/Administration/Manor01/`; the settlement map contains 44 modular placements. `Launch_Manor01.command` opens that settlement. The hall, roof, veranda, gate, walls, side building, stonework, garden and banners remain separate reusable pieces. Shrine lanterns and SHŌEN materials are reused.

The user-directed revision replaces the original flat roof treatment, adds deeper gate joinery and reused-crest curtains, darkens structural timber, replaces regular masonry and rebuilds the pine foliage as volumetric needle clusters.

Obvious visual limits: the rounded fieldstone and pine crowns remain more stylized than the reference, and the wall/roof repetition is still visible. Settlement terrain is the existing prototype presentation, without the reference's finished landscape dressing. No claim of matching the reference's final visual quality.

Views: `artifacts/manor01/manor-three-quarter.png` is the Blender source render; `unreal-three-quarter.png` and `unreal-settlement.png` are actual rendered Unreal editor captures. No gameplay changes, project tests or performance measurements were made.
